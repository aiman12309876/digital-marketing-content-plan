# Social Media Content Plan - DecodeLabs Digital Marketing Project 1

## 1. Brand Overview
- **Brand Name:** TechLearners
- **Industry:** EdTech (Online Learning)
- **Mission:** Making AI and technology education accessible to everyone

---

## 2. Target Audience

### Primary Audience
| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Age | 18-28 years |
| Gender | All |
| Location | Pakistan (Urban Areas) |
| Education | University students, Fresh graduates |
| Interests | AI, Python, Career Growth, Freelancing |
| Pain Points | Expensive courses, No guidance, Unclear career path |
| Platforms | Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube |

### Audience Persona: "Ayesha - The CS Student"
- 22 years old
- BS Computer Science student
- Wants to learn AI but can't afford expensive courses
- Follows tech influencers on Instagram
- Active on LinkedIn for job opportunities

---

## 3. Content Pillars

### Pillar 1: Educational Content (40%)
- **Goal:** Teach AI concepts in simple way
- **Examples:**
  - "What is Machine Learning?" explained in 60 seconds
  - Python tips and tricks
  - AI tools you can use for free

### Pillar 2: Career & Growth (30%)
- **Goal:** Help students build careers in tech
- **Examples:**
  - How to get your first tech internship
  - Top AI skills for 2026
  - Resume tips for fresh graduates

### Pillar 3: Inspiration & Motivation (20%)
- **Goal:** Inspire students to pursue tech
- **Examples:**
  - Success stories of Pakistani tech professionals
  - Student testimonials
  - "You can do it" motivational posts

### Pillar 4: Behind the Scenes (10%)
- **Goal:** Build trust and connection
- **Examples:**
  - Day in the life of an AI student
  - Learning journey updates
  - Team introductions
